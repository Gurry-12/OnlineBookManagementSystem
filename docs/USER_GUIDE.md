# User Guide - Whispering Pages

Welcome to the Whispering Pages User Guide. This document explains how to use the various features of the Book Management System.

## Getting Started
### Registration
To join Whispering Pages, click on **Sign Up**. You'll need to provide your name, email, and choose a role. 
*Note: Admin and Super Admin accounts require manual approval by the existing administrator.*

### Login
Enter your credentials on the **Login** page. Upon successful login, you will be redirected to your specific dashboard based on your role.

## Managing Your Library
### Browsing Books
Visit the **Explore Books** section to see our full catalog. Use the search bar to find specific titles or authors, and use categories to filter results.

### Viewing Details
Click on any book card to see its full description, ISBN, publication date, and stock status.

## Shopping & Orders
### Adding to Cart
On any book list or detail page, click the **Add to Cart** button. You can view your current items and total by clicking the cart icon in the navigation bar.

### Checkout
Review your items in the **Shopping Cart** and click **Proceed to Checkout**. Fill in your shipping details to complete the order.

### Order History
View all your past purchases in the **My Orders** section of your dashboard.

## User Profile & Favorites
### Favorites
Click the heart icon on any book to add it to your personal favorites list. This makes it easy to find books you're interested in later.

### Profile Settings
Manage your account details and security settings from the **Profile** section.
